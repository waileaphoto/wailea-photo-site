The Legend of Hana, Maui

Journey along 52 miles of winding rainforest coast, 600 curves, and 54 historic bridges.

1. The Journey to East Maui

The Hana Highway is one of the world's most famous scenic drives. Traveling from Kahului to the isolated town of Hana takes you through dense rainforests, past cascading waterfalls, and alongside rugged Pacific ocean bluffs.

2. Cascading Rainforest Waterfalls

Along the route, dozens of freshwater streams cascade down from the slopes of Haleakalā. Highlights include Twin Falls, Waikani Falls (Three Bears), and the iconic Wailua Falls near mile marker 45.

3. Keʻanae Peninsula & Black Sand Coastlines

Stopping at the Keʻanae Peninsula offers panoramic views of dark volcanic basalt rock meeting the crashing surf of the Pacific Ocean, framed by taro fields that have been cultivated for centuries.

4. The Pipiwai Trail & Bamboo Forest

Located in the Kipahulu section of Haleakalā National Park beyond Hana town, the Pipiwai Trail leads hikers through a towering bamboo forest to the 400-foot Waimoku Falls.
