Solomon Balter

Photographer, Wailea Photo

SECOND GENERATION

A cinematic eye, shaped on Maui.

Solomon Balter is a second-generation Wailea Photo photographer, born and raised on Maui, known among the family team for a cinematic style. He learned the craft directly from his father, founder Laurence Balter.

Solomon photographs family portraits, couples sessions and weddings across Wailea, Makena and South Maui as part of the family-run Wailea Photo team.

WHY CLIENTS BOOK SOLOMON

Local roots, cinematic style.

Solomon was raised on Maui and trained by his father in the same approach that has defined Wailea Photo for more than 25 years, with an added focus on movement, atmosphere and cinematic composition.

Like every Wailea Photo session, Solomon's sessions are shot personally — never handed off to an outside contractor.
