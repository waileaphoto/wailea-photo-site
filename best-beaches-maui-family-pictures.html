Best Beaches in Maui for Family Pictures

Beautiful water is only the beginning. A field guide to the light, wind, access and breathing room that make a beach work for a real family.

4,500+ sessions
25+ years on Maui
By Laurence Balter

The short answer

There is no single “best” beach.

Best overallMokapu
Young childrenMaluaka
Turquoise waterKanahā
SunriseKeawakapu

After photographing more than 4,500 family sessions across Maui over the past 25 years, we have learned that the beach with the bluest water is not always the best choice.

Maui has no shortage of beautiful beaches. But light, wind, crowds, accessibility, shoreline conditions and how safely children can explore often have a greater effect on the finished photographs than scenery alone.

Most families understandably prefer portraits without tourists in the background. Complete privacy on a public beach can never be guaranteed, but the right combination of location, timing and careful camera positioning can greatly reduce distractions.

These four beaches offer the most dependable balance of beautiful scenery, favorable light, manageable crowds, convenient access and variety within a small area. They are not the only beautiful beaches on Maui. They are the places we trust for particular kinds of family sessions.

Our field rankings

Four beaches.
Four distinct strengths.

The right location depends on the people in front of the camera, the hour of the day and the kind of memory they want to make.

01

Mokapu BeachWailea · Best overall & sunset

Mokapu, in front of the Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort, offers ocean views, lava rock, palms, open sand and neighboring coastal scenery within a compact area. That variety creates several distinct looks without asking a family to travel between locations. Near sunset, warm directional light adds dimension to faces and richness to the shoreline.

The most versatile all-around choice for a classic Wailea family portrait session.

02

Maluaka BeachMakena · Children, grandparents & large families

Maluaka gives children room to move, play and interact instead of remaining posed in one place. The beach supports candid moments and more formal portraits, while offering enough working space for extended families, reunions and legacy photographs. Its flexibility also makes it a strong setting for intimate weddings and elopements.

Our most versatile choice for toddlers, grandparents, large groups and intimate ceremonies.

03

Kanahā BeachKahului · Turquoise water & privacy

Kanahā can produce exceptionally vivid turquoise water in favorable morning light. Its open coastal setting often offers more visual separation from resort crowds and fewer buildings in view. Airport activity and trade winds can vary, so conditions and timing matter more here than at a sheltered resort beach.

The strongest choice when vivid water and an open, less resort-oriented landscape are the priorities.

04

Keawakapu BeachSouth Maui · Best for sunrise

At sunrise, Keawakapu is generally quieter, cooler and often less windy than it will be later in the day. The long shoreline gives us room to work and minimize distractions. Young children may also be fresher before a full day of swimming, sightseeing and sun.

One of South Maui’s best combinations of soft light, convenience and quiet morning conditions.

Beyond the postcard

What makes a beach photograph well?

01

Light

The same beach looks entirely different at sunrise, noon and sunset. Early and late light is usually softer and more flattering than midday sun.

02

Wind

Wind affects hair, clothing, comfort, ocean texture and even how easily a family can hear direction. Maui’s trade winds often strengthen later in the day.

03

Crowds

Timing matters, but so do angles, lenses and background separation. A public beach can appear quiet in the finished frame even when other people are present.

04

Water color

Turquoise water depends on sunlight, weather, wind, the ocean floor and the direction of view. No beach is equally blue under every sky.

05

Safety

Shore break, currents, rocks, tides and seasonal conditions matter—especially for young children and older family members.

06

Accessibility

Shorter walks and stable footing reduce stress for toddlers, grandparents, pregnant mothers and anyone with limited mobility.

A note on access

Commercial photography permits

Professional portrait, wedding and elopement photography at participating state-managed Maui beaches requires authorization through the State of Hawaiʻi Department of Land and Natural Resources, commonly known as a DLNR WikiPermit.

Wailea Photo obtains permits through the State of Hawaiʻi WikiPermits system for the specific beach, date and activity. Not every beach uses the same process: some are state-managed, others fall under Maui County, and resort-adjacent areas may involve additional property restrictions.

Kāʻanapali should not be treated as a standard WikiPermit beach. Because ownership and jurisdiction along that shoreline can be more complicated, commercial photographers should verify the exact location and authorization before scheduling a session.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is the best beach in Maui for family pictures?

Our best overall choice is Mokapu Beach in Wailea because it offers varied scenery, convenient access and beautiful sunset light. We prefer Maluaka for young children, grandparents and large groups; Kanahā for turquoise water and an open setting; and Keawakapu for sunrise.

What is the best Maui beach for families with toddlers?

Maluaka is our preferred choice because it provides room for natural movement and several settings without requiring a long walk. Keawakapu at sunrise is another strong option for families staying in Wailea or South Kihei.

Which Maui beach has the bluest water?

Kanahā can produce exceptionally vivid turquoise water under favorable morning light. Water color changes with weather, wind, sunlight and camera angle, so no beach looks exactly the same every day.

Is sunrise or sunset better for family pictures?

Sunrise is generally quieter, cooler and often less windy. Sunset produces warmer, more dramatic light but may bring larger crowds, stronger wind and tired children. The best choice depends on your family’s schedule, children’s ages and preferred style.

Can you guarantee an empty beach?

No photographer can guarantee complete privacy on a public beach. We reduce distractions through location selection, timing, careful angles and thoughtful positioning.

Can we photograph at the beach in front of our hotel?

Possibly, but not every resort beach is equally suitable. It may be crowded, face the wrong direction for your preferred light, offer limited variety or involve property restrictions. We evaluate the exact beach and time before confirming it.
