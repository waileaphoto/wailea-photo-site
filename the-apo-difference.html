THE APO LENS, ANSWERED

What clients ask about this optic.

What does "APO" mean in a camera lens?

APO stands for apochromatic — a lens design that corrects color fringing across the full spectrum of light, so images stay sharp and true-color even in high-contrast conditions.

Why does this matter for photography in Maui specifically?

Maui's midday sun and deep-red sunset light are exactly the conditions that cause ordinary lenses to show color fringing and softened detail. An apochromatic lens holds accurate skin tones and sky color even when shooting straight into the light.

Is the Leica APO lens used for every Wailea Photo session?

Clients can choose to have their session photographed with the Leica APO-Summicron lens. Mention your interest when you book so we can confirm it for your session.

Can I request the Leica APO lens for a specific session type?

Yes. It can be requested for sunrise, sunset, family, couples or wedding sessions — anywhere true color and clarity in bright or backlit light matter most.

Does requesting the Leica APO lens change the price of my session?

Lens selection is discussed as part of session planning. Let us know your interest when you book and we'll confirm the details for your specific session.
