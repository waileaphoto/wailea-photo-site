PLANNING YOUR MATERNITY SESSION

A calm, unhurried maternity session on the beach.

Comfort comes first

We keep maternity sessions gentle: short walks on soft sand, plenty of rest between setups and direction that flatters without asking you to hold awkward poses.

The best light for maternity

The Last Half of Sunset brings warm golden tones and flowing silhouettes, which is why it is our favorite for maternity portraits. Morning sessions are cooler and calmer if you prefer to avoid the afternoon heat.

Where we photograph

Sunset sessions meet at Maluaka Beach in Makena, a quiet crescent of sand with soft evening light. Morning sessions meet at Wailea Beach beside the Four Seasons, which is calm and beautifully lit early in the day.

Partners and siblings

Partners and children are always welcome, and we make time for portraits of you alone, the two of you together and the whole family.

Your photographs

Every image is edited by hand for natural color and brightness, and delivered within 24 to 72 hours as high-resolution files with full print rights. We do not smooth skin or reshape bodies; you will look like yourselves.

MATERNITY PHOTOGRAPHY, ANSWERED

What expecting parents ask us first.

How much does a Maui maternity photography session cost?

Maternity portraits are not a separate session — choose any Sunrise or Sunset session and simply mention it's for maternity photos when you book.

What is the best trimester for a Maui maternity photoshoot?

Most clients book between 28 and 36 weeks, when the belly is well shaped but travel and standing for a session are still comfortable.

Sunrise or sunset for maternity photos?

Both work beautifully. Sunrise gives fresh, soft color with fewer crowds; sunset gives warmer, more dramatic light.

Can partners and other children be included?

Yes. Many maternity sessions include partners and older siblings as part of the family's growing story.

Do you offer an outfit change during the session?

Many sessions can include time for an outfit change — just mention it when you book so we can plan accordingly.

Where do Maui maternity photo sessions take place?

Sunset maternity sessions are at Maluaka Beach in Makena and morning sessions are at Wailea Beach next to the Four Seasons.

How soon will we receive our maternity photos?

Edited, high-resolution images are delivered within 24 to 72 hours, with full print rights.
