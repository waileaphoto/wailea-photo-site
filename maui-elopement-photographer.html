How much does a Maui elopement cost with Wailea Photo?

Our all-inclusive elopement package is $2,599, tax included. A $100 deposit secures your date and the remaining balance is $2,499. It includes a Native Hawaiian officiant, a bouquet, leis and wedding arch, 1.5 hours of photography with 200+ edited images delivered within 24–48 hours, the beach permit, General Liability Insurance and live streaming for family at home.

Can we elope with just the two of us?

Yes. The package is the same whether it is just the two of you or a small group: up to 15 guests are included at no extra cost.

Who marries us, and who handles the marriage license?

Derick Sebastian, our Native Hawaiian officiant, performs the ceremony and takes care of the Hawaii marriage license. A consultation with him is arranged once your deposit is paid.

Can family watch from home?

Yes. Live streaming is included: we set up your phone on our tripod with our microphones so family and friends can watch your vows live.

Where on Maui can we elope?

We photograph elopements on the beaches of Wailea, Makena and South Maui, including Maluaka Beach in Makena. Your beach permit is included.

What if it rains or it is cloudy?

If it rains, we will attempt to reschedule. Clouds are welcome: they soften the light and skin tones, and some of our most beautiful images are made when it is partly cloudy.

Can we bring guests or chairs?

Up to 15 guests are included and additional guests are $20 each, up to 30 on the beach or 100 on private property. Chairs are not allowed on the beach, only on private property.

How do we book?

Book online with a $100 deposit to secure your date. The remaining $2,499 balance completes the package.
