A Thoughtful Guide to Maui Lūʻau

Nestled in the heart of the Pacific, Maui is a tropical paradise that beckons travelers with its stunning landscapes, vibrant culture, and enchanting luaus.

BY LAURENCE BALTER · FOUNDER & LEAD PHOTOGRAPHER, WAILEA PHOTO

AN EVENING OF STORY

How to choose an experience that feels welcoming, memorable and connected to place.

Dance, adornment & the stories carried through movement
CULTURAL TRADITIONS

A Feast for the Senses

Lūʻau are not simply evening events; they bring together food, music, dance, storytelling, and hospitality. This guide considers the different experiences offered around Maui and what may matter most when choosing one for your family.

Whether you are seeking ancient historical storytelling in Lahaina, lush waterfall gardens in West Maui, or chic modern oceanfront dining in Wailea, Maui’s luaus provide an unforgettable evening under the Pacific stars.

Strength, expression & cultural storytelling
TRADITION & WATERFALLS

Old Lahaina & Wailele Polynesian Luau

1. Old Lahaina Luau: Embracing Tradition

The Old Lahaina Luau stands as a beacon of authenticity, drawing inspiration from the rich history and cultural tapestry of Hawaii. Nestled in Lahaina, this luau invites guests to step back in time with its traditional Hawaiian feast, complete with imu-roasted pig, poi, and other local delicacies. The evening is complemented by captivating hula performances and live music, creating an immersive experience that transports visitors to a bygone era.

2. Wailele Polynesian Luau: Waterfall Wonderland

Nestled in the West Maui Mountains, the Wailele Polynesian Luau at The Westin Maui Resort & Spa offers a unique twist to the traditional luau setting. Surrounded by lush greenery and overlooking a cascading waterfall, this luau captivates guests with a stunning natural backdrop. The evening includes cultural performances, a delectable buffet, and the chance to witness the mesmerizing stories of the Polynesian people unfold under the starlit Hawaiian sky.

Grace, rhythm & an evening of hula
WAILEA ELEGANCE

Grand Wailea & Andaz Luau

3. Grand Wailea Luau: A Luxurious Affair

For those desiring a touch of luxury, the Grand Wailea Luau at the Grand Wailea Resort is the epitome of opulence. Known for its world-class amenities, this luau combines the island's flavors with elegant entertainment. Guests can indulge in a sumptuous buffet featuring locally sourced ingredients and relax in the lap of luxury as they enjoy the breathtaking sunset over Wailea Beach.

4. Andaz Luau: Modern Elegance in Wailea

Situated at the Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort, this luau seamlessly blends traditional Hawaiian elements with a modern, chic vibe. The feast here is a fusion of classic Polynesian flavors and contemporary culinary artistry. The oceanfront setting, coupled with fire dancers and live music, creates an atmosphere that's both sophisticated and authentically Hawaiian.

CONCLUSION

Gateways to the Soul of Hawaii

Maui's luaus are not just events; they are gateways to the soul of Hawaii. From the traditional charm of Old Lahaina to the luxurious celebrations in Wailea, each luau tells a unique story of culture, cuisine, and community. As you embark on this cultural journey, let the rhythm of the hula, the aroma of imu-roasted pig, and the warmth of Hawaiian hospitality transport you to a world where every moment is a celebration of the aloha spirit.
