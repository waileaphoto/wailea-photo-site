Your Family Photographer at the Andaz Maui at Wailea

We photograph on Mokapu Beach — the sand directly in front of your resort — every evening at sunset. A short walk from your room, one all-inclusive price, and no resort commission added on top.

BY LAURENCE BALTER · FOUNDER & LEAD PHOTOGRAPHER, WAILEA PHOTO

STEPS FROM YOUR ROOM

The beach in front of your resort is our studio.

The Andaz Maui at Wailea sits on fifteen oceanfront acres along Mokapu Beach — and that same stretch of sand, just past the resort’s boardwalk, is where we photograph families every evening. There’s no rental car to arrange and no drive across the island. When your session time comes, you simply walk down to the beach, and we take care of the rest.

For guests staying at the Andaz, that ease is the whole point. Spend your afternoon at the pool, the spa or Ka’ana Kitchen, get ready in the comfort of your own room, and stroll out to meet us as the light turns gold.

Where we meet Mokapu Beach, on the sand fronting the Andaz Maui at Wailea (3550 Wailea Alanui Drive). From the public beach parking beside the resort, walk toward the outdoor showers, follow the ramp down past the Andaz, and cross the boardwalk to the sand. We’ll send the exact meeting spot and the evening’s sunset time when you book.
ONE PRICE, BOOKED DIRECT

All-inclusive — with no middle-man markup.

When a photographer is arranged through a resort or concierge desk, a commission is almost always folded into what you pay. We’re different. Wailea Photo is an independent, family-run studio, so you book directly with us — and the price you see is the entire price, with no concierge fee or third-party markup added on top.

Every session is all-inclusive: one flat rate covers the shoot and all of your naturally edited, high-resolution images, delivered in a private online gallery within about 48 hours — no per-image charges and no upsells afterward. And every session is photographed personally by one of the Balters, never an outside contractor. Sessions start at $299.

QUICK ANSWERS

What Andaz guests ask us.

Do you photograph right at the Andaz Maui at Wailea?

Yes. We meet you at Mokapu Beach — the public beach directly in front of the resort — and photograph there every evening at sunset. It’s a short, easy walk from the resort down to the sand.

Do I have to book through the resort’s concierge?

No. We’re an independent, family-run studio, so you book directly with us at one all-inclusive price. Nothing is added on top — no concierge fee and no commission.

How much is a session, and what’s included?

Sessions start at $299, all-inclusive. That one price covers the shoot and every one of your naturally edited, high-resolution images, delivered in a private online gallery within about 48 hours.

What time will we meet?

We photograph around sunset, when the light over Mokapu Beach is at its best, so your exact start time shifts with the season. We’ll confirm the evening’s sunset time and where to meet as soon as you book.
