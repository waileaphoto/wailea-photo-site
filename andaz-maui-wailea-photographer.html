Photography for Andaz Maui Guests

We photograph on Mokapu Beach — the sand directly in front of your resort — every evening at sunset. A short walk from your room, all-inclusive with no concierge markup.

BY LAURENCE BALTER · FOUNDER & LEAD PHOTOGRAPHER, WAILEA PHOTO

STEPS FROM YOUR ROOM

Fifteen oceanfront acres along Mokapu Beach, captured at the finest light of the day.

Mokapu Beachfront fronting the Andaz Maui
CONVENIENCE FIRST

The beach in front of your resort is our studio.

The Andaz Maui at Wailea sits on fifteen oceanfront acres along Mokapu Beach — and that same stretch of sand, just past the resort’s boardwalk, is where we photograph families every evening. There’s no rental car to arrange and no drive across the island. When your session time comes, you simply walk down to the beach, and we take care of the rest.

For guests staying at the Andaz, that ease is the whole point. Spend your afternoon at the pool, the spa, or Ka’ana Kitchen, get ready in the comfort of your own room, and stroll out to meet us as the light turns gold.

Where we meet Mokapu Beach, on the sand fronting the Andaz Maui at Wailea (3550 Wailea Alanui Drive). From the public beach parking beside the resort, walk toward the outdoor showers, follow the ramp down past the Andaz, and cross the boardwalk to the sand. We’ll send the exact meeting spot and the evening’s sunset time when you book.

QUICK ANSWERS

What Andaz Maui guests ask us.

Where do you photograph — right in front of the Andaz?

Yes. Mokapu Beach, directly in front of the resort, is our home base — we photograph there most evenings of the week. For Andaz guests that means no drive and no meeting point to find: you walk down from your room and we are already there.

Why photograph at the Andaz rather than another Wailea beach?

The lava rock. Most Wailea beaches are open sand with nothing to sit on, which limits you to standing poses. The rocks in front of the Andaz give us natural places to seat a family, so we can vary the poses across a session instead of repeating the same line-up in different spots.

What is the shot between the two large lava rocks?

It is the frame we are known for, and the one on the front page of this site. The two large rocks in front of the Andaz sit far enough apart to hold a family between them and frame the sunset behind. We photograph it on most sessions here — it is the single strongest reason we keep coming back to this beach.

Can we have dinner nearby afterwards?

Easily. Morimoto Maui is at the Andaz, steps from where we photograph, so a lot of families book a table for straight after the session while everyone is still dressed for it. Sunset sessions end at sunset, which lines up well with a dinner reservation.

Do I have to book through the resort’s concierge?

No. We’re an independent, family-run studio, so you book directly with us at one all-inclusive price. Nothing is added on top — no concierge fee and no commission.

How much is a session, and what’s included?

Every session is all-inclusive: one price, quoted when you book, covers the shoot and every one of your naturally edited, high-resolution images, delivered in a private online gallery within about 48 hours.

What time will we meet?

We photograph around sunset, when the light is at its best, so your exact start time shifts with the season. We’ll confirm the evening’s sunset time and where to meet as soon as you book.
